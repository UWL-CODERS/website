About Us

What is CODERS?

CODERS is a community of UWL students scattered across a variety of departments who share common interests of coding, technology, and computer science.

Our Mission

CODERS is committed to enriching the La Crosse community by volunteering, teaching fundamental computer science skills, supporting student development and career readiness, and fostering diversity and inclusion in technology.

How Can I Join?

If you are a UWL Student, becoming a member is easy! Simply sign up through UWL's myOrgs platform to start receiving our weekly emails. You can also join our Discord server to connect with other members to stay updated on events!

Join Us in 3 Simple Steps:

  1. 1

    Visit the CODERS club page on myOrgs or click the Join button below!

  2. 2

    Click the "Join" button and Log In with your UWL Credentials.

  3. 3

    Start Receiving Weekly Emails About Meetings and Upcoming Events!
